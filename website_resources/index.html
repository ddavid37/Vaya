Why it matters

Life doesn't always come with a 36-month plan. You move cities, take a new job, spend a season somewhere new, and suddenly you need a car without the commitment that usually comes with one.

Buying feels like too much. Leasing locks you in. Short-term rentals were designed for weekend trips, not everyday life. There's never been a comfortable middle ground for people who just need a car for a while.

That's exactly what Vaya is. A car that fits your life right now: flexible, straightforward, and priced to make sense.
